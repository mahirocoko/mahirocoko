# Owner Taste Needs Positive Grammar, Not Only Guardrails

**Date**: 2026-07-15  
**Tags**: frontend-design, taste, mac-aesthetic, personal-projects, evidence, skill-authoring

## Lesson

A frontend-design skill can be rigorous about evidence, provenance, responsive behavior, and anti-cloning while still producing work that feels generic. Guardrails prevent bad transplantation; they do not create a compelling positive direction.

When an owner explicitly approves a strong aesthetic preference, preserve it as a scoped authority source rather than stripping it from the reusable method.

## Durable Pattern

For confirmed Mahiro personal projects where material repo/brand direction is thin:

1. Keep explicit product, repo, accessibility, design-system, and approved-reference evidence first.
2. Apply the owner-approved Mac product aesthetic as a strong prior, not as repo truth or corpus-derived doctrine.
3. Route by surface job:
   - presentation surfaces prioritize proposition, proof, and acquisition;
   - working surfaces prioritize repeated tasks, object/state relationships, and direct manipulation.
4. Require executable derivation:
   - identify the primary object;
   - identify its characteristic transformation/state/environment/result;
   - let that relationship determine composition or workspace geometry;
   - name the generic composition rejected.
5. Keep the result original: no Apple/third-party trade dress, fake native chrome, generic glass, or platform cues without a product job.
6. Validate Thai, contrast, non-color state meaning, focus, text zoom, responsive proof/task role, and non-default states.

## Authority Boundary

Personal scope must be explicit in Mahiro's statement, repo-local docs, or trusted project memory. A repository namespace, package author, remote owner, or local path alone is insufficient. Client, employer, team, third-party, and uncertain-ownership work must not inherit the personal fallback.

## Why This Matters

The earlier Macapp Supply workflow promoted valuable proof and corpus methodology but intentionally left Mac visual vocabulary out of the packaged skill. That was correct as research hygiene but incomplete as owner taste. The 2026-07-15 correction separated the two cleanly: historical corpus findings remain bounded, while Mahiro's explicit personal preference becomes an owner-approved profile with stronger evidence still able to override it.
