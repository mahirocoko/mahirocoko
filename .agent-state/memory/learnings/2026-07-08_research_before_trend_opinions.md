# Research before trend opinions

**Date**: 2026-07-08
**Tags**: research, workflow, loop-engineering, communication

## Lesson

When Mahiro asks about current AI-industry trend or role terms such as “loop engineer,” do not answer from intuition first. Research the exact term and credible adjacent sources, then separate: what sources explicitly say, what is inferred from adjacent practices, and what my own synthesis/recommendation is.

## Evidence

Mahiro asked for my view on “loop engineer.” I answered from a plausible internal synthesis, then Mahiro corrected: “ใจเย็น research มาก่อนตอบสิ”. I then researched credible adjacent sources before answering again: Anthropic effective agents, HumanLayer 12-factor agents, LangChain/Simon Willison context engineering, Hamel evals, and OpenAI Agents SDK.

## Behavior change

For emerging AI terms, role labels, model workflow claims, or tooling trend opinions:

1. Search current/official/primary sources first.
2. Identify whether the exact phrase is established or mostly social/hype language.
3. Use adjacent credible sources only with clear labels.
4. Give a grounded opinion after evidence, not before.

## Reuse trigger

Use this lesson for questions about AI roles, model/provider workflows, agent-engineering methods, industry terminology, or “people are talking about X” prompts.
