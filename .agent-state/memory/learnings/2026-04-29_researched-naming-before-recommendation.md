# Lesson Learned: Research Brand Names Before Recommending Them

**Date**: 2026-04-29  
**Tags**: branding, naming, research, product-incubation, paoplew

## Lesson

For brand and product naming work, do not recommend a name from vibe alone. Run at least a basic conflict scan before presenting any name as a recommendation.

## Why It Matters

The initial `Flowmint` direction felt semantically strong for a cashflow app, but research showed direct conflicts across finance, software, crypto, domains, and public repos. That made the name impractical despite the good fit. The better workflow emerged after the user explicitly asked that every recommendation include research. From that point, naming became more grounded: candidates were rejected because of evidence, not taste, and `PaoPlew` became stronger because it survived a basic public scan while also meeting readability and tone constraints.

## Apply Next Time

- Treat unresearched names as ideas only, not recommendations.
- For any recommended brand or feature name, include exact and near-match research notes.
- Check web, domains, GitHub/package ecosystems, app stores when relevant, and local market language variants.
- Label the result as basic conflict research, not legal trademark clearance.
- Balance collision risk with readability; a highly coined name is not automatically good if users cannot say it naturally.
