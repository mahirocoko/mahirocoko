# Laya MLX Lab — Character IP Explore

Status: historical explore contract. The six raw candidates were completed unchanged; Mahiro later selected the A1 centered subtle-3D adaptation. Current chronology is in `../README.md`, and the human selection is owned by `A1-adapt-centered-3d/identity-selection.md`.

## Product truth

Laya MLX Lab is a private research/blog archive for experiments with small local Laya decision models on Apple Silicon. Each chapter carries a question, method, reproducible evidence, reports, and media. Dino is Chapter 1, not the whole identity. The mascot should feel like a calm, curious research companion for an evidence-first archive: lovable, ownable, simple at 32x32, and not a game mascot or generic AI assistant.

W1 wordmark exploration is separate historical evidence. Do not use W1, C1/C2 logo candidates, or any previous generated image as a reference. Do not create a logo, wordmark, app icon, badge, UI mockup, or full webpage in this explore route.

Avoid: dinosaurs/reptiles, moths/insects, brain, circuit board, neural network, robot, stock mascot, generic AI assistant, borrowed Interfaces.dev branding, text, watermark, scientific symbols, and game-studio cues.

## Shared character budget

One complete character per square canvas. Build one dominant continuous outer silhouette from roughly 4–7 large soft shapes. Use at most two broad internal character-color regions plus one solid background. Two simple eyes and one tiny mouth only if useful. Keep paired identifying features visible. Remove texture, thin fragile lines, sharp tips, tiny decoration, glossy hotspots, strong 3D rendering, cast shadows, and scenery. Use thick rounded contours, compact proportions, soft cheeks, calm friendly posture, and extremely subtle depth only. The character must remain readable at 32x32.

## Six candidates

Each direction has two materially different compositions. Do not mirror a composition mechanically.

- A1 Archive Sprout: small soft bookmark/leaf-like archive companion, lower-left anchored with generous open space above/right; broad warm cream and muted sage on deep navy.
- A2 Archive Sprout: same subject family but side-cropped on the right edge with face fully visible and negative space on the left; broad parchment and moss-green on warm charcoal.
- B1 Signal Pebble: rounded pebble companion with one broad embedded trace ribbon as the defining feature, low centered on a deep blue field with open upper space; broad stone cream and electric blue.
- B2 Signal Pebble: same subject family placed high-left with the trace ribbon bending into the body, open lower-right space; broad lilac stone and muted orange on near-black plum.
- C1 Tiny Lantern: soft compact lantern-like companion with one warm core as the defining feature, upper-right placement with open lower-left space; broad ink navy and warm amber on dusty indigo.
- C2 Tiny Lantern: same subject family emerging from the lower edge, face and paired side handles visible, open upper space; broad charcoal and pale gold on dark forest blue.

## Output

One raw full-bleed 1:1 PNG per candidate, solid background, no transparency requirement. Preserve first returns unchanged. Prompts and receipts belong in the candidate folders. Make one contact sheet only after all six raw files exist. At this Explore phase, no identity had been selected yet; the later human selection is a separate Adapt/handoff phase.
