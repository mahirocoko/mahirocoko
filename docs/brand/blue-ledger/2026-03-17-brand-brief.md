# Blue Ledger Brand Brief

Date: 2026-03-17
Status: Working brand brief for generated skill input

## Brand Name

Blue Ledger

## Product Summary

Blue Ledger is a simple personal income and expense tracker for one person. It helps the user log daily financial activity quickly, see current balance immediately, and review recent entries without navigating through a complex dashboard.

## Product Scope for V1

- single-user personal finance tracking
- one-screen experience
- quick add form for income and expense
- summary cards for `Balance`, `Income`, and `Expense`
- recent activity list
- browser-only persistence with `localStorage`

## Audience

Individuals who want a clean and trustworthy place to track daily money movement without the weight of business accounting software.

## Positioning

Blue Ledger should feel calm, sharp, and reliable. It is not a budgeting coach, not an accounting suite, and not a playful habit app. It is a compact personal finance surface that feels premium without feeling corporate.

## Reference Posture

Primary visual reference:
- `https://stripe.com/`

Interpretation rule:
- use Stripe as a mood and quality reference only
- borrow the sense of structure, restraint, and confidence
- do not imitate Stripe's product composition, enterprise density, or brand voice directly

## Visual System

Color direction:
- white and near-white surfaces
- blue as the main emphasis color
- gray neutrals for metadata and dividers
- avoid loud gradients and saturated multi-color accents

Surface direction:
- clean cards
- subtle borders
- soft shadows
- medium radius

Layout direction:
- airy, deliberate spacing
- clear hierarchy
- mobile and desktop both first-class

## Voice and Copy

Language posture:
- Thai-first
- selected English labels are allowed where they increase clarity or polish

Tone:
- calm
- precise
- non-judgmental
- useful

Avoid:
- overly cheerful fintech copy
- motivational clichés
- aggressive CTA language
- jargon-heavy accounting phrasing

Example label posture:
- `ยอดคงเหลือ Balance`
- `รายรับ Income`
- `รายจ่าย Expense`
- `รายการล่าสุด`
- `เพิ่มรายการใหม่`

## Interaction Behavior

- data entry should feel immediate
- summaries should update right after save
- the product should reduce friction, not add process
- empty states should gently prompt the first action
- no hidden complexity in `v1`

## Anti-Patterns

- dark-heavy UI
- noisy chart dashboards
- crypto-style neon finance aesthetics
- enterprise admin tables
- playful sticker-like illustration systems
- gamified saving language
