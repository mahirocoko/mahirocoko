## 3. Compact Crop & Product Role Notes

### A. Product Role & Simulation Independence
- **Source Asset Truth**: The generated image serves as pure, unmanipulated photographic source material. It contains no embedded split line, slider thumb, reticle, badges, or telemetry overlays.
- **Color Profile Demonstration**: The visual contrast between Display P3 (Wide Gamut) and sRGB IEC61966-2.1 is achieved through client-side CSS/color-profile simulation across the Optic Blade seam, not baked into the image.
- **Cross-Surface Consistency**: Identical asset used across both the marketing landing page card (1040×480) and the desktop macOS utility inspection canvas (440×388).

### B. Crop Safety & Compositional Stability
- **Upper-Third Silhouette Anchor**: The jagged alpine mountain crest is anchored in the upper third of the frame, ensuring peak silhouettes survive both wide desktop 16:10 viewports and narrower 4:3 or centered mobile vertical viewports without head-chopping or loss of context.
- **Reflective Base**: The calm glacial lake occupies the lower-middle field, providing a stable, calm reflection pool that balances the top-heavy mountain geometry.
- **Framing Elements**: Conifer pine trees and weathered granite boulders are constrained to the lower borders and peripheral shelves, framing the view naturally without impinging on the central vista.

### C. Central Seam Clearance
- **Unobstructed Midline**: The central vertical seam across 50%–55% horizontal position is maintained completely free of isolated vertical tree trunks, jarring rock spires, or asymmetric high-frequency focal distractions.
- **Bilateral Comparison Legibility**: The Optic Blade hairline can traverse smoothly across the central water surface, tree line, and mountain saddles with optimal visual clarity.

### D. Tonal Gradient (Warm-Left to Cool-Right)
- **Left Quadrant**: Rich golden-hour sun angle cresting the ridge; amber, apricot, and honey-gold warm atmospheric glow, rim lighting on peaks, and warm specular water glimmers. Maximizes wide-gamut (P3) saturation visibility.
- **Center Transition**: Smooth, continuous natural gradation through neutral mountain daylight.
- **Right Quadrant**: Crisp, cool alpine twilight; slate-blue sky, deep navy water reflections, and muted neutral/slate rock shadows. Provides neutral baseline for standard sRGB comparison.

### E. Negative Constraints Adherence
- **Strictly Excluded**: Zero UI badges, telemetry text, labels, logos, watermarks, frames, browser chrome, reticles, Optic Blade sliders, people, hikers, structures, celestial planets, or unnatural HDR halo artifacts.
