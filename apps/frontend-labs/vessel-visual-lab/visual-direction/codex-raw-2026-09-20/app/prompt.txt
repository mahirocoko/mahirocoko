Single compact macOS utility window (680px width by 440px height aspect ratio) centered horizontally and vertically on a neutral dark slate desktop surface (#121214). Straight-on 2D UI presentation, perfectly orthographic with no 3D tilt, no angled perspective, and no surrounding monitor bezel.
