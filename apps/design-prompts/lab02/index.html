Materials with documentation behind them.

Sustainability is handled as engineering evidence. The E1 uses recycled structure where it improves efficiency, then brings traceable tactile materials into the cabin.

  • Recycled body structure 34% recycled aluminum supports lower process impact while removing weight from the platform.
  • Traceable textile batches Seat and door surfaces use documented yarn sourcing and automotive-grade abrasion testing.
  • Bio-composite trim Matte cabin surfaces use a lignin-based composite instead of old-growth wood veneer.
  • Production notes Paint and joining steps are documented in the owner sustainability addendum where data is available.
