01 · Materials

Proof you can see—and specifications you can trace

Sustainability here is not a slogan. It is a set of measurable choices: recycled aluminum where it supports lightness, responsibly sourced cabin materials where you spend your time, and manufacturing steps selected to reduce process emissions where feasible without compromising structural integrity.

  • Recycled aluminum in the body-in-white 34% recycled content helps lower manufacturing impact while shaving mass—a direct contribution to efficiency and handling.
  • Traceable textiles on primary touchpoints Seat and door surfaces use traceable yarns; durability is validated to premium automotive-grade wear standards.
  • Interior wood alternative Open-pore finishes use a lignin-based composite—warmth without old-growth dependency, paired with soft-touch matte sealers.
  • Assembly with lower-emission processes where viable Paint shop and body join steps target reduced VOC paths; final numbers ship in the sustainability addendum.

03 · Cabin

A calm interior, precisely instrumented

The cabin prioritizes acoustic comfort, tactile materials, and clarity of information. Assistance features are integrated as support—not spectacle—so attention stays on the road.

Interface hierarchy

Primary driving data remains in direct line of sight; secondary controls move to a haptic-assisted center module to reduce glance time.

Driver assistance

Highway assist uses camera and radar fusion with conservative cut-in behavior; hands-on monitoring with clear escalation when attention drifts.

Acoustic refinement

Laminated side glass and targeted insulation yield low cabin noise at motorway speeds—paired with tactile woven inserts on primary touch surfaces.
