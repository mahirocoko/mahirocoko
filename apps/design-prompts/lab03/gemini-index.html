02 Approach

Scenes are built for play first.

Writing, audio, level blocking, and accessibility checks move together from the first internal build. We treat environmental storytelling as a system, relying on rooms, roads, and weather systems to carry clues before a character explains them.

Chapter two route memory and alternate storm calls
