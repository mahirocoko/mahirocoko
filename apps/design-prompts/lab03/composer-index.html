Studio approach

We work small and keep builds honest: if a scene is not playable, it does not ship to a milestone. Narrative systems are tied to place and memory—routes change, objects linger, radio calls overlap—before characters spell anything out.

Environmental storytelling first

Lighting, props, and audio cues carry plot weight. Exposition exists, but only after the room has already taught you how to read it.

Prototype-led production

Graybox beats slide decks. Each milestone ends in a runnable slice with objectives, controller notes, and a short test pass for subtitles and remapping.

Accessibility and localization early

Nine-language subtitle plan is budgeted before content lock. Contrast passes and UI scale land in the same weeks as VO integration—not as a late polish bolt-on.

Art direction and sound as one lane

A six-person core team keeps visual and audio direction in the same conversations so mood stays consistent when weather, time of day, or radio static shifts.
